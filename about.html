About Riverside Art Center

Welcome to the Riverside Art Center!

As a proud 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization, we were founded with one joyful mission in mind: to spark imagination and inspire creativity through the arts. Right in the heart of Historic Downtown Wapakoneta, our welcoming space serves as a creative home for artists, art lovers, and curious beginners alike. Whether you are stopping by to admire local talent or looking for a new hobby, our doors are always open to help our community connect and grow.

We love bringing people together through our lively lineup of hands-on classes, community events, and rotating gallery exhibits. From painting and stained glass to special artist receptions, there is always something exciting happening inside our walls. No matter your age or skill level, we invite you to stop in, say hello, and "Let Your Creativity Flow" with us!

Music Park @ Belcher Park

Our love for creativity doesn't stop at our front door—we are also thrilled to sponsor Music Park @ Belcher Park! Because we believe the arts belong to everyone, supporting this wonderful outdoor space lets us share the joy of music with neighbors and families across Wapakoneta. It is just one more way we help fill our community with rhythm, laughter, and inspiration all year long.

Stop by Belcher Park to enjoy the playground and explore the colorful, flower-shaped outdoor musical instruments sponsored by the Riverside Art Center!

Gather your friends and family to make some music together at Belcher Park's interactive outdoor sound garden.

Take a book or leave a favorite read at the charming, hand-painted Tiny Library located right at Belcher Park.
